Measurement units and claim boundary:

- Main and rolling predictors use synthetic calibrated hints and injected errors. The mechanism is compiler/runtime integrated, while trained real-workload Rust classifier coverage and application throughput remain open.
- Rolling byte-epochs are sums across logical observation boundaries, not wall-clock MiB-seconds.
- There is no same-host LLAMA or TEMERAIRE implementation in this evidence, so no direct cross-paper superiority percentage is claimed.
