Claim boundaries
- Raw timing and statistical-superiority claims stay within the UniAlloc semantic or allocator-neutral probe family.
- Cross-family comparison is limited to side-by-side within-pair effects, actual backing footprint, and deployment mechanism.
- gperftools uses explicit pre-reserved HugeTLB and is distinct from THP and Google TCMalloc HPAA.
- snmalloc has no public allocator THP toggle; this is a process-eligibility sensitivity control.
- This campaign injects 5% false-long and 5% false-short prediction noise; its observed 4.993% classification failure is a synthetic robustness control, not empirical compiler accuracy.
- This campaign validates actual runtime backing. Runtime-confirmed classifier accuracy is separate evidence; the prior 4.990% to 1.263% epoch result also uses synthetic injected error.
- Maximum/steady effective resident are process-residency metrics; they do not directly measure allocator fragmentation.
- CPU frequency was not fixed, CPU 15 was not isolated, and hardware performance counters were unavailable (perf_event_paranoid=4).
